### AFSL Compliance Policies Drafting

An AFS licensee has various regulatory compliance obligations under the financial services laws and its AFSL conditions. Non-compliance of these obligations could have serious consequences including the suspension or cancellation of the AFSL.
A robust AFSL compliance framework starts with the drafting of AFSL compliance policies and procedures having regard to the nature, size and complexity of your particular business.
When to consider getting new policies drafted?
A new licensee that has just been granted a new AFSL should consider having compliance policies and procedures drafted. While you would have submitted a number of proof documents such as B3 Compliance Arrangements, B3 Arrangements of Conflicts of Interest Management, B7 Risk Management System Statement, and a few other documents, these may not comprehensively cover all the key compliance requirements that you need to take into account when providing financial products and services.
It may also be worthwhile to consider having compliance policies drafted when the existing policies and procedures have become stale, i.e., they have not been reviewed or updated over an extended period of time so that it may be more efficient and cost effective to have fresh documents drafted rather than reviewing and updating the existing ones.
Coverage
The precise coverage will depend on your requirements. Our AFSL Manual is typically designed to cover your key obligations under the Corporations Act and your AFSL conditions. This may include (non-exhaustive):
- Human resources and financial resources
- Appointment, training and termination of representatives and responsible managers
- Monitoring and supervision of representatives
- Conflicts of interest management
- Breaches and breach reporting
- Risk management
- Promotional material management
- Client money handling
- Internal and external dispute resolution
- Outsourcing
- Record-keeping
- Compensation arrangement
- Registers
Our Approach
You may be able to purchase template policies online. Why should you consider working with us?
It really depends on what you are looking for. What is important is that your policies and procedures align with the nature, size and complexity of your business. Templates that can be easily purchased online are typically well-considered and comprehensive documents that take into account a wide variety of scenarios.
